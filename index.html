Ancient Rome · 161–180 CE
Meditations
Marcus Aurelius
A Roman emperor's private journal of Stoic self-examination, written in Greek during military campaigns. Meditations is not a systematic philosophy but a practice — the emperor reminding himself daily how to live: with virtue, reason, and acceptance of the natural order. It is the most intimate record we have of a ruler trying to be a good person.
"You have power over your mind, not outside events. Realize this and you will find strength."
